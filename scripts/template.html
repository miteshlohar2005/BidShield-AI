End-to-End Compliance Pipeline

How BidShield AI Works

From raw bid ingestion to final stamped officer decision, every step is transparent, deterministic, and securely auditable.

01. Intake

Tender & Bid Ingestion

Tender technical specifications, budget benchmark, and bidder commercial submissions are securely loaded.

02. Registry Verification

Cross-Database Validation

Cross-referenced against 10 national registry adapters (GSTN, ITD, MCA21, Udyam, EPFO, ESIC, Debarment list).

03. Rules Engine

15 Deterministic Checks

Mathematical evaluation of EMD, turnover, pricing deviations, Make in India thresholds, and document completeness.

04. Collusion Detection

Pairwise Tender Screening

Screening for clustered pricing (<0.5%), rapid submission timing (<5 min), shared registered addresses, and cover bids.

05. AI Synthesis

Reasoning Layer

Sarvam AI (or deterministic fallback) synthesizes deductions into plain-language officer briefings without altering scores.

06. Officer Sign-off

Binding Officer Decision

Qualified, Clarification, Hold, or Non-Compliant recorded with role verification, written override justifications, and audit stamps.

Deterministic Rulebook

The Fifteen Compliance Checks

Each check returns PASS, FLAG, or FAIL with verified numbers and direct source citations. PASS earns full weight, FLAG half, FAIL zero.

⚠
Critical Hard-Cap Rule:

A failure on EMD compliance, Statutory documents, GST registration, or Debarment status automatically caps the maximum score at 45/100 and triggers a Critical Risk classification. Any other failure caps the score at 72/100 (High Risk).

Anti-Cartel Screening

Pairwise Collusion Intelligence

Every bidder on a tender is algorithmically compared against every other to flag suspicious coordinated bidding patterns.

Price Clustering

Flags quotes that land within 0.5% of each other, signalling artificial price coordination.

Submission Proximity

Flags tender bids uploaded within 5 minutes of each other from related participants.

Shared Corporate Addresses

Identifies ostensibly distinct bidders that share registered office premises or industrial units.

Cover Bidding

Detects bids priced >25% above the benchmark, designed solely to create a false impression of competitive bidding.

ⓘ
Important Disclaimer:

Collusion indicators are algorithmic warning signals intended to alert the procurement officer to request clarifications or conduct enhanced due diligence. They do not constitute legal proof of cartelization.

Key operational and regulatory details regarding BidShield AI.

The model receives only the tender title, specification parameters, bidder identity, and the deterministic results of the 15 code-evaluated checks. It never receives commercial bank accounts, raw tax passwords, or unmasked sensitive PII, and it cannot alter computed scores or verdicts.
All 15 checks, compliance scoring, and collusion algorithms run in pure deterministic code. If the AI service is unreachable, BidShield AI automatically falls back to an offline rule-based synthesis and clearly labels the recommendation as deterministic.
